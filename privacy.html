Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy describes the PDFRilo editor included in this website release. It distinguishes document processing on your device from ordinary website connections.

Your documents

The editor reads, renders, edits and exports PDFs locally in your browser. It does not upload PDF contents or added images to PDFRilo servers. Documents and editing history are held in temporary browser memory. Opening another document resets that editing session; closing the tab ends it. Downloaded files remain wherever you save them on your device.

Website requests

Your browser requests website files and PDF libraries from the site hosting provider. Hosting providers may receive standard request information such as IP address, browser information, timestamps and requested URLs, and may keep security or operational logs. If deployed on Cloudflare Pages, Cloudflare supplies hosting and network infrastructure. This release self-hosts its PDF libraries and fonts.

Analytics, advertising and cookies

This release includes no analytics trackers, advertising scripts, account system or document cloud storage. The editor does not set tracking cookies. Hosting infrastructure may have its own security practices. If analytics or advertising are added later, this policy and any required controls should be updated before those services are enabled.

Problem reports

The current contact form prepares a report locally and lets you copy it. Sending is not connected, and a report is not submitted by preparing or copying it. Do not include passwords, financial details or sensitive PDF contents. No PDF upload is included in the support form. If a real submission service is connected in a future release, the page and policy must explain where reports are sent and how they are handled.

Security and your choices

Keep your browser updated, keep a copy of important documents, and inspect downloaded PDFs before relying on them. To discard the current session, close the tab or open a different PDF. Local processing does not prevent other software or browser extensions on your device from accessing information.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised date. For the current support status and reporting options, visit the Contact page.
