Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This policy explains document processing on your device, ordinary website connections, analytics and contact messages.

Your documents

The editor reads, renders, edits and exports PDFs locally in your browser. It does not upload PDF contents or added images to PDFRilo servers. Documents and editing history are held in temporary browser memory. Opening another document resets that editing session; closing the tab ends it. Downloaded files remain wherever you save them on your device.

Website requests

Your browser requests website files, PDF libraries and fonts from our hosting provider. PDFRilo is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare provides hosting and network infrastructure and may process standard request information such as IP address, browser information, timestamps and requested URLs for delivery, security and operational purposes. PDFRilo self-hosts its PDF libraries and fonts.

Analytics, advertising and cookies

PDFRilo uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand website visits and page performance. Its browser beacon sends page-view and performance metrics to Cloudflare. These analytics requests do not include your PDF contents or added images.

Cloudflare describes Web Analytics as privacy-first analytics that does not collect or use visitors' personal data. Website hosting and security request processing are separate from Web Analytics. Learn more in the Cloudflare Web Analytics documentation.

The current editor has no advertising scripts, account system or document cloud storage. It does not set tracking cookies. Hosting infrastructure may have its own security practices. If these services change, we will update this policy.

Suggestions and problem reports

When you submit the Contact or Help Us Improve form, the message, optional name, email and browser/device details you enter are sent through Web3Forms to PDFRilo's contact inbox. Web3Forms receives the submission and may process technical request information to deliver messages and prevent abuse. See the Web3Forms Privacy Policy for its practices.

Your PDF and added images are not attached or uploaded by this form. We use your message to respond and improve PDFRilo. Please do not include passwords, financial details or sensitive PDF contents. To request deletion of a message, contact us using the form and identify the message.

Security and your choices

Keep your browser updated, keep a copy of important documents, and inspect downloaded PDFs before relying on them. To discard the current session, close the tab or open a different PDF. Local processing does not prevent other software or browser extensions on your device from accessing information.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted here with a revised date. For questions or reporting options, visit the Contact page.
